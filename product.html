Made to be worn every day

The Ember Runner Low is the shoe we reach for most. It starts with a recycled-foam midsole that stays springy from the morning commute to the evening walk, tuned over eighteen prototypes to sit right between cushioned and grounded.

The upper is full-grain leather from a Leather Working Group–certified tannery, backed with a breathable mesh lining so your feet stay cool. A moulded heel counter locks you in without breaking in, and the rubber outsole is siped for grip on wet pavement.

We kept the silhouette clean on purpose — it reads just as easily with denim as it does with tailored trousers. Weighs 268g per shoe in a US 9, so you forget you’re wearing them.
